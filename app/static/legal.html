This report is informational only. It is not a legal determination, not a certified VPAT, and not a substitute for a human WCAG/EAA audit.

What Accesspack produces

Accesspack fetches a public HTML document and runs automated accessibility rules (axe-core when Node is available, otherwise a conservative HTML-only subset). It then drafts a VPAT 2.5-shaped report and an accessibility statement. Those artifacts are starting points for a conversation with qualified auditors and counsel.

What Accesspack does not produce

  • A certified VPAT or a Section 508 determination
  • A WCAG conformance claim
  • An EN 301 549 conformity assessment or European Accessibility Act legal opinion
  • Coverage of authenticated pages, PDFs, mobile apps, or JavaScript-rendered DOM
  • Legal advice

Acceptable use

Only submit public http(s) URLs you are allowed to request. We block private and reserved addresses, localhost, and cloud metadata endpoints. We do not execute page JavaScript in the Python process.
